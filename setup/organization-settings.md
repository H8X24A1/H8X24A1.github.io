# Organization Settings

In the [HXA.io Portal](https://portal.hxa.io), open **Account > Organization Profile**. The page shows the organization's customer number and separates contact information, company details, branding, and emergency settings.

## Primary Contact (Owner)

This section displays **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, and **Language**. The contact identity fields may be read-only.

The user menu under **Account > Users** includes **Set as a Primary contact**. Availability and the requirements for changing the primary contact must be checked for your account. Contact HXA.io Support if you need to clarify ownership or cannot change the contact through the portal.

## Company information

| Field | Information to maintain |
| --- | --- |
| Company name | Official organization name |
| Street, Zipcode, City, State, Country | Company address |
| Floor, Building etc. | Additional address details |
| Phone, Fax | Organization contact numbers |

Update the relevant fields and use **Save and Update** for this section. Required fields are marked with an asterisk in the portal.

## Branding settings

Upload the organization's logo by clicking the upload area or dragging an image onto it. The portal specifies a maximum image size of **1 MB** and a **2:3 width-to-height ratio**.

Resource background images are configured separately. See [Room Booking Background Images](/setup/resource-setup/room-booking-setup/background-image-setup.md).

## Emergency and safety settings

The **First Aid** section provides an **Enable** switch for First Aid helpers and a **Select a group** selection. Choose the intended group and use this section's **Save and Update** button when configuring the feature.

Groups are managed under **Account > Groups**. See [User Setup](/setup/user-setup.md#groups).

## Check the result

After saving a section, reopen the page and check that the intended values are retained. Confirm logo appearance and First Aid presentation in the application where you use them.
